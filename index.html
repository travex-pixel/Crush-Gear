Two machines. One deck. Knock them off, break them down, or flip them over.

Choose your machine

How to play

Steer

Drag anywhere on the left of the screen, or use WASD. Push the way you want to go; the machine turns to follow. With assisted steering, letting go makes your machine hold a fighting distance and face the rival.

Strike

Tap for a ram. It hits hardest when you're lined up and moving. Keep holding after the ram to build a spin, then release to lunge. A missed ram leaves you open for a moment.

Guard

Hold to brace: hits to your front do far less damage and knockback. Press it just before a hit lands to parry and stagger the attacker. Tap and release quickly to dodge sideways.

Special

The yellow gauge fills as you fight. When the button glows, press it. Each machine's finisher can be dodged or parried, so pick your moment.

Win a round

Push the rival off either open end of the deck, break one of its three parts (front weapon, body or wheels), or hit it while it's knocked down to flip it. If time runs out, the healthier machine wins, unless it's pinned against a barrier. First to two rounds.

Angles matter

Hits to the side wreck wheels and hits to the rear wreck the body. Every ram also wears down your own front weapon, so spamming it costs you.
